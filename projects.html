Machine Learning · 2025

Reproducing Grokking Phenomena

Reproduced grokking phenomena on modular addition using a decoder-only Transformer built from scratch. I also investigated how training-data ratios, model architectures, optimizers, individual Transformer components, and loss landscapes shape the transition from memorization to generalization.

Network Science · 2026

Urban Traffic Network Robustness

First Prize, 23rd Jiang Zehan Mathematical Modeling Competition · Peking University · Top 10%

Modeled urban transportation systems as graphs, established random-attack baselines, and developed adaptive greedy attack strategies that were up to eight times as efficient as the random baseline. I also designed corresponding repair strategies based on betweenness centrality.

Political Science · Research Assistance

Authoritarian Regime Resilience

Supported Prof. Yanjun Liu’s research through data collection, cleaning, variable construction, descriptive statistics, and regression analysis. I conducted a detailed analysis of the factors associated with authoritarian regime resilience using Cox proportional hazards models. The findings contributed to Ordering Loyalties: Hierarchical Authoritarianism and Governance in China and Beyond (forthcoming, Palgrave Macmillan) by Prof. Liu.
