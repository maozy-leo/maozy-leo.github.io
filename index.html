Welcome

Hello, I’m Zhiyuan (毛志远).

I am an undergraduate at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, majoring in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics with a focus on Financial Mathematics. I am also pursuing a second degree in Economics at the National School of Development, Peking University.

My interests lie in statistics, machine learning, and their applications in scientific problems—from theoretical foundations of machine learning to the use of LLMs in social sciences. I am also interested in econometrics and quantitative finance.

Currently, I’m working with Prof. Ruixun Zhang to design algorithms of diffusion models with a focus on their theoretical properties and practical applications in scientific problems.
